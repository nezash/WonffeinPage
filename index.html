✧ Commission Terms ✧

Terms of Service

Updated Jan 23, 2026. Starting a commission means you've read and agree to these.

  • You must be 18 or older to commission NSFW/+18 work. Everything else is minor-friendly.
  • Commissions are generally first come, first served.
  • The process can take anywhere from five days to one–two months, depending on order and complexity. If it's running slower than expected, Won will reach out right away.
  • Private commissions that can't be posted by Won include a fee of 50% of the base price.
  • .PSD (source) files are available at 45% of the base price.
  • Edits made by others are allowed with the client's approval.
  • Please don't commission if you're at financial risk — repeated immediate refund requests will result in being blacklisted.
  • Won has the right to reject any order she's not comfortable drawing, for any reason.

  • Full payment upfront.
  • Refunds follow VGen's refund policy.
  • Please make sure you're happy with the piece and have no financial issues before buying a digital good.
  • Insisting on a full refund after this warning will lead to being blacklisted.

  • Starts with a sketch of the main idea.
  • Illustrations are usually 2000×2000px at 300dpi by default — let Won know if you want a different size or resolution.
  • You'll get a watermarked sketch first, for your approval.
  • Once the sketch is approved, you'll get constant WIPs through whichever platform you contacted her on.
  • Design, pose and expression need to be locked in before payment — a change of mind after a full sketch costs 20% of the total price for a new one.
  • 5 free revisions included, with screenshots at each step so you can point out what needs changing.
  • Changes beyond the sketch stage cost $5–$30 depending on complexity.
  • Mistakes on Won's part (design or anatomy) are fixed free of charge.

  • Delivery takes between 3 days and 2 months.
  • Any delays are communicated as soon as possible, through whichever platform you prefer.

  • Illustrations are for your personal use only — you can't profit from them in any way.
  • Commercial usage is priced at 200% of the personal/non-commercial price.
  • Commercial usage means commissioned art used for merch or any direct profit. Stream-related use is generally fine.
  • As the client, you fully own the image you bought for personal use.
  • Please credit @Wonffein (all socials) when using commissioned work.
  • Unless otherwise agreed, Won retains the right to publish and use the commissioned piece (portfolio, promo, making-of, etc.) — with your permission, your social handle can be credited too, unless an NDA was paid for.
  • Profiting from Won's work without a mutual agreement is prohibited and illegal.
  • Using Won's personal, commissioned, existing, Patreon-exclusive or merch designs for crypto assets or AI is strictly prohibited.
  • Tracing or editing commissioned work isn't permitted.

  • Available through Discord and VGen chat.
  • Discord: wonffein
